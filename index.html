House rules
  • Be kind. No bullying, no naming people, no private details about anyone.
  • No student IDs, phone numbers or anything that points to a person.
  • Lanterns get a random nickname. There are no accounts or names.
  • If you are in danger, use the numbers in the Library, not the wall.

    Exam-day checklist

    Saved on this device.

      Study without burning out

      • Quiz yourself instead of rereading. Pulling answers out of memory sticks better.
      • Spread it out. Several short sessions across days beat one long night.
      • Protect last night's sleep. A rested brain recalls more than two extra hours of cramming.
      • Start with ten minutes on the scariest topic.

      AI companion

      Talk to Ze

      Ze is a gentle companion, not a therapist. Good for untangling a hard day, trying a calming exercise or planning one small next step. Chinese or English is fine.

      Connect a real AI (optional)

      Without a key, Ze uses pre-written replies so the site works anywhere. Add your own key to let Ze talk through ChatGPT or Grok. The key stays in this browser only. Use a key with a low spending limit and never on a shared computer.

      Messages are not stored. Please do not share names or student ID numbers.
